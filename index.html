About关于我

Hi, thanks for stopping by!

您好，感谢您的到访！

I am currently Associate Professor in the School of Statistics at Renmin University of China. As a researcher, my work is focused on solving problems at the intersection of statistics, economics, and finance, in the Blockchain × Digital Assets × Digital Finance space.

我目前在中国人民大学统计学院担任副教授。我的研究聚焦于统计学、经济学与金融学的交叉前沿，尤其致力于探索区块链 × 数字资产 × 数字金融领域的核心问题。

Before moving to China, I worked as Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. Previously, I completed my PhD in Financial Mathematics from the University of Manchester, where I was advised by Prof. Saralees Nadarajah and worked on statistical distribution theory with applications to finance. I also worked as a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the School of Mathematics at the University of Manchester, funded by a US Army Research Laboratory Short-Term Innovative Research Grant.

赴华任职之前，我曾在马德里卡洛斯三世大学统计系担任讲师。在此之前，我在曼彻斯特大学师从 Saralees Nadarajah 教授，取得金融数学博士学位，主要从事统计分布理论及其金融应用方面的研究。此后，我留在曼彻斯特大学数学学院担任博士后研究员，所承担课题获美国陆军研究实验室短期创新研究项目（STIR）资助。

Away from research, I am particularly interested in how technology and AI influences culture and society.

研究之余，我对技术与人工智能如何影响文化与社会亦抱有浓厚的兴趣。
